ENGINEERING LOG

Design Decisions

01
Aluminium housing

Selected to reduce overall structural mass while retaining sufficient rigidity for the prototype.

02
Modular bearing support

Chosen to simplify maintenance and allow bearing replacement without redesigning the complete housing.

03
Separate rotor chambers

Used to maintain adequate clearance between the two counter-rotating assemblies.
