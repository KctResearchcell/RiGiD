Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

RiGiD ("we", "our", or "the application") is a project management and personal productivity web application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use RiGiD.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, RiGiD may process the following information:

  • Account information associated with your authentication, such as your email address and basic profile information.
  • Information that you voluntarily enter into RiGiD, such as projects, study information, papers, prototypes, journals, reminders, and other content.
  • Information required to provide features that you explicitly choose to use, such as Google Drive integration.

2. Google Sign-In

RiGiD may use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. When you choose to sign in with Google, Google may provide RiGiD with basic account information permitted by the authentication process, such as your name, email address, and profile information.

RiGiD does not receive your Google account password.

3. Google Drive

RiGiD may provide optional Google Drive functionality. If you authorize Google Drive access, RiGiD may access only the Google Drive information and permissions required by the features you use and authorize.

You can revoke Google Drive access through your Google Account settings.

4. Supabase

RiGiD uses Supabase for services such as authentication and data storage. Information associated with your RiGiD account and the data you save in the application may be processed and stored through Supabase.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by RiGiD may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain RiGiD.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Store and retrieve user-created content.
  • Provide optional integrations requested by the user.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the application.

6. Data Sharing

RiGiD does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services used to operate RiGiD, including authentication, database, hosting, and integrations that you choose to use.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed through RiGiD. However, no internet-based application can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You may choose whether to use optional integrations such as Google Drive. You may also revoke previously granted Google permissions through your Google Account.

9. Children's Privacy

RiGiD is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RiGiD's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or RiGiD's handling of information, please contact the RiGiD developer through the contact information provided with the application. student.research@kct.ac.in
