About RiGiD and how to use it

1. What is RiGiD?

RiGiD is a project management and personal productivity workspace designed to help users organize, manage, and track their academic, research, technical, and creative work in one place.

RiGiD brings different types of work together into a single workspace, allowing users to keep track of their progress, activities, projects, and personal development.

Depending on your role and access, RiGiD provides either an administration environment or a personal workspace.

2. Before You Sign Up

Important:

At present, do not use your KCT email address to create your RiGiD account. KCT email configuration is not currently available for RiGiD.

For now, use your personal Google account and personal email address when signing up.

3. How to Sign Up

Step 1 — Choose Sign Up with Google

From the RiGiD login page, choose Sign Up with Google.

Step 2 — Select Your Personal Google Account

Sign in using your personal Google account. At this stage, do not use a KCT email account, since KCT email configuration is not currently available.

Step 3 — Confirm Google Drive Access

RiGiD uses Google Drive to provide storage for your RiGiD work. During the Google authorization process, you will be asked to confirm the requested access to your Google Drive account.

Review the permission shown by Google and confirm it if you want to use RiGiD's Google Drive integration.

RiGiD-created work can then be associated with your own Google Drive account under the RiGiD folder.

4. Access Request and Admin Approval

Step 4 — Your Access Request Is Sent

After you complete Google sign-up and authorize the required Google Drive access, your RiGiD access request is sent to the administrator responsible for your RiGiD environment.

Step 5 — Wait for Approval

Your account does not immediately receive access to the main RiGiD workspace.

The administrator must review and approve your request before you can enter the appropriate RiGiD workspace.

Remember:

Completing Google sign-up does not by itself mean that you have been approved to use the RiGiD workspace. Access is granted after the appropriate administrator approves your request.

5. What Happens After You Are Approved?

Once your access has been approved, RiGiD determines the interface you should use based on your assigned role.

There are two main experiences:

  • Administrator — receives access to the administration environment.
  • Normal User — receives access to their Personal Workspace.

6. Administrator Interface

For Administrators Only

Administrators have access to the RiGiD administration environment rather than being limited to their own personal workspace.

The administration interface is intended to provide the administrator with an overall view and control of the RiGiD environment.

This includes managing and reviewing the users, access requests, organizational relationships, and other administrative information available to the administrator.

Administrators can also access a member's personal workspace when permitted by the application. When an administrator views another member's workspace, that workspace is presented as a read-only view.

7. Normal User Interface

Personal Workspace

Normal users are taken to their own Personal Workspace after they are approved.

The Personal Workspace is where you can view and manage your own progress and activities.

Your workspace can contain information such as:

  • Your profile and personal information
  • Your projects and work
  • Study activities
  • Papers
  • Projects
  • Prototypes
  • Designs
  • Simulations
  • Reminders
  • Daily journal/blog entries
  • Accolades and achievements
  • Useful personal links

8. Creating and Managing Work

From your Personal Workspace, you can create different types of work according to the features available to your account.

When creating a work item, RiGiD can record information such as its title, description, category, status, start date, end date, and its associated forum, team, or domain where applicable.

Your workspace then uses this information to display your work through categories, timelines, calendars, and progress-related views.

9. Google Drive Storage

RiGiD integrates with Google Drive so that work created through RiGiD can be associated with your own Google Drive account.

When you authorize Google Drive access, RiGiD can use the permitted Google Drive functionality to create and organize RiGiD-related files or folders for your work.

The intended storage location is your own Google Drive account, under the RiGiD folder, rather than a shared Google Drive account owned by RiGiD.

10. Your Personal Workspace

Your Personal Workspace is intended to represent your own progress and activities.

Normal users manage their own workspace. They cannot use the personal workspace URL to freely access another member's workspace.

Administrative access is different. An administrator may be permitted to view another member's workspace for administrative purposes. When doing so, the member's workspace is displayed in read-only mode.

11. Forums, Teams, and Domains

RiGiD can organize users and work through forums, teams, and domains.

Where applicable, you can request to join a forum and select the relevant team or domain available to you.

Once you become a member of the appropriate workspace, your related work can be associated with that forum, team, or domain.

12. RiGiD at a Glance

  1. Open RiGiD.
  2. Use your personal Google account to sign up.
  3. Review and confirm the requested Google Drive permission.
  4. Your access request is sent to the appropriate administrator.
  5. Wait for the administrator to approve your access.
  6. After approval, RiGiD directs you to the interface corresponding to your role.
  7. Administrators use the administration dashboard.
  8. Normal users use their Personal Workspace.
  9. Normal users can create and manage their own work, track progress, maintain activities, and use the available RiGiD tools.
  10. Google Drive is used as the user's own storage location for RiGiD-related Drive content.

13. Need Help?

If you encounter a problem with signing up, Google authorization, access approval, or your RiGiD workspace, contact the administrator responsible for your RiGiD environment. student.research@kct.ac.in
